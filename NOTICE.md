## Xbox Micro modifications

This fork adds and maintains:

- a native SwiftUI macOS application and menu-bar controller;
- an embedded OpenMicro/Node.js runtime with `.app` and `.dmg` packaging;
- machine-readable engine lifecycle, controller, action, and Agent-state events;
- six populated, cyclic controller layers with Chinese interaction presets;
- editing for all supported controls and action parameters;
- Chinese workflow prompt defaults, import/export, reset, and safe legacy migration;
- Codex Hooks, Accessibility, login-item, engine-path, and runtime diagnostics;
- process-supervision cleanup and cross-language desktop/engine contract tests.
